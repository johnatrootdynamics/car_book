Browser privacy

Cookies & browser storage

Updated November 15, 2026

Essential only

Track Ops does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Reading the homepage, Features page or these policy pages does not require a new Track Ops session cookie. Features such as sign-in, shopping carts and protected forms use essential storage to work.

The cookie notice is informational. Dismissing it does not authorize advertising, analytics or any additional tracking.

Your controls

There are no optional tracking cookies to enable or reject. You can reopen the notice using Cookie settings in the footer. To remove website cookies or prevent all storage, use your browser’s privacy controls. Blocking essential storage can prevent sign-in, checkout and protected forms from working.

Track Ops does not enable optional tracking when a browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal; the same essential-only behavior applies to every visitor.

Services you choose to open

Payments, digital waiver signing and social sign-in may open a separate provider’s page or an embedded service when you use those features. That provider may use its own cookies or storage. Track Ops cannot remove another provider’s cookies; review its privacy information and your browser controls.

The public overview video, fonts, icons and scanner library are served with the Track Ops website. Watching the overview does not load a third-party video player.

Cookies are only part of privacy

Account information, purchases, waiver records and server records are separate from browser cookies. Clearing cookies does not delete those records. See our Privacy Policy for how information is used and how to request help.
